Bio

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC), starting in Spring 2027, where I will be advised by Prof. Xiaoming Liu.

I received my master's degree from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2025, with research conducted at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Prof. Xiangyu Zhu and in close collaboration with Prof. Zhen Lei. I received my bachelor's degree from the University of Science and Technology Beijing in 2022, where I worked closely with Prof. Rui Wang.

I greatly enjoyed my time as a visiting research student at Westlake University in 2026, under the supervision of Dr. Anpei Chen.

My current research interests focus on scene reconstruction and understanding, and digital human modeling and interaction.
